# The two videos

YC asks for a **one-minute founder video** (required) and lets you attach a
**demo video** (optional — attach one; you have something to show, and most
applicants don't).

---

## 1. Founder video — 60 seconds

Rules that actually matter: all founders on camera, one continuous take,
faces visible, phone camera is fine, no slides, no music, no editing. They
are not judging production. They are judging whether you two are real and
whether you can say what you do in a sentence.

**Language:** English. If Karen's English is not comfortable, he speaks his
part in Russian and you subtitle it — that reads as authentic, not as a
problem. Do not have Anna speak for him.

### Script

**Anna (0:00–0:12)**

> Hi, we're Anna and Karen, and we build Python Method. Karen has spent
> thirty years rebuilding bodies wrecked by cancer treatment. I turned his
> practice into a platform.

**Karen (0:12–0:32)**

> In 2024 my mother got sick, stage four. I sat next to her and saw it:
> the illness damages you from one side, the treatment from the other, and
> nobody's job is the second one. I promised her I would help everyone who
> came to me. I have done four hundred and eighty cases since — by hand,
> in WhatsApp.

**Anna (0:32–0:52)**

> Four hundred and eighty is what one man's hands hold. So the platform
> does everything except his judgement: it talks to people at any hour in
> their language, builds the case file from their lab results, drafts his
> replies, and flags an emergency to him in seconds. It never interprets
> anyone's results — that answer is his. It's live, people pay us through
> it, and it costs seventy dollars a month to run.

**Both (0:52–1:00)**

> We want to make him ten times bigger without making him worse. That's
> what we're doing at YC.

### Notes

- The 480 number does the most work in the whole minute. Say it slowly.
- Do not say anything about beating cancer, remission, or results against
  the disease. Not in the video, not in the application, not on the site.
  It is untrue as a claim, it is illegal to publish in California, both
  payment processors ban it outright, and someone could delay treatment
  because of it.
- Rehearse until it fits in 60 seconds without hurrying. If it doesn't
  fit, cut Anna's last line, not Karen's mother.

## 2. Demo video — 2 to 3 minutes

A screen recording of the real site, your voice over it, no slides. Order,
because it is the order of the argument:

1. **The landing page (15s)** — Russian and English, live URL visible in
   the address bar the whole time.
2. **The guest AI (30s)** — ask it something about the centre and let it
   answer. Then ask it *"what do my test results mean?"* and let it refuse
   and send you to Karen. That refusal is the most important twenty
   seconds in the video — it is the whole regulatory and product thesis in
   one interaction.
3. **Onboarding → case (25s)** — questionnaire, upload a document, show
   the case appearing.
4. **The cabinet (25s)** — case status, documents, the chat with a voice
   message in it.
5. **The staff panel (40s)** — red flags at the top of the page; open a
   case; show the case-aware assistant drafting a reply; show Karen adding
   a line to the knowledge base and the assistant using it.
6. **Payment (15s)** — the offer checkbox, Stripe, and the webhook record
   landing in the admin.

Say the numbers out loud as you go: $1,440 and $3,675, $50–70 a month of
infrastructure, ~$1 of AI per active client.

Use a demo case with invented data. Never record a real client's
documents, name or messages — not for YC, not for anyone.
